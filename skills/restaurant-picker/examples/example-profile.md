# Taste profile: Sam (fictional example)

> Everything in this file is invented, including the person and every restaurant, to show the format. Copy it to `taste-profile.md` in the skill folder and replace it with your own, or ask the skill to build one with you.

## Basics

- Home area: Riverside District, Lakeview City
- Language for replies: English
- Rating scale: 1 to 10. Would go back to anything 5 or above.
- Tier thresholds for the self-test: 8.5+ = likely to love, 5 to 8.4 = worth a try, below 5 = likely to disappoint.

## Principle (in Sam's own words)

"I want food that tastes like someone cared about every component. I'll happily eat simple food, but not lazy food."

### Green flags (what makes Sam love a place)

- Every dish tastes distinct, not built on the same base sauce. Example: **Copper Lantern** (each small plate had its own character).
- A side or condiment that is clearly made in-house. Example: **Nonna Vee's** (house chili oil and focaccia).
- A humble dish done better than anywhere else. Example: **Early Bird Diner** (best hash browns in town).
- Small, low-key places where the owner obviously sweats the details. Example: **Kaito Counter** (eight seats, each piece seasoned differently).

### Red flags (what disappoints Sam)

- Sweet-leaning sauces or glazes. Example: **Golden Wok Express** (everything tasted like the same sweet sauce).
- Great ingredients with ordinary seasoning. Example: **Prime Cut House** (excellent beef, bland and over-salted sides).
- Hype and long lines without substance. Example: **The Viral Dumpling**.
- Slow pacing that lets food arrive lukewarm. Example: **Atelier Nord**.

### Other notes

- Food matters far more than service; great service can't save mediocre food.
- Spice is welcome; sweetness in savoury food is not.
- Doesn't care about travel time.
- Values famous places for shared conversation: if not yet visited, a well-known place can move ahead of lesser-known "worth a try" places.
